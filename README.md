# muse-fortuneliq-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c).

## The entry

> You will lose everything twice: once to the market, and once explaining leverage to your mother at Sunday lunch.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it keeps the oracular fortune voice, puts both the trader and the loss in the line, and the second "loss" - explaining leverage to your mother - is a fresh, warm turn that makes the liquidated trader laugh rather than cry.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
